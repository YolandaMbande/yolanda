Hello, stranger.

I’m Yolanda, a software developer with a background in Computer Science and an interest in building practical, well-designed digital solutions.

My experience spans full-stack development, working with technologies such as PHP, Laravel, JavaScript and Java. I’ve worked on web applications, developed features, resolved bugs and collaborated on projects where functionality and usability go hand in hand.

I enjoy learning, experimenting with new technologies and continuously improving the way I approach development.
